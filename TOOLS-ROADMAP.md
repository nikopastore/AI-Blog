## Article Format

Each tool article includes:

### Structure (600-1,000 words)
- **What is [Tool]?** - Clear explanation
- **Why Businesses Choose It** - Key benefits
- **Business Applications** - Real use cases
- **Cost Structure** - Pricing breakdown
- **ROI Example** - Concrete calculations
- **Getting Started** - Practical steps
- **Integration Ecosystem** - How it connects
- **vs Alternatives** - When to choose what
- **When It Makes Sense** - Decision framework

### Business Focus
- Non-technical language for leaders
- Practical ROI calculations
- Real company examples
- Implementation timelines
- Cost-benefit analysis
